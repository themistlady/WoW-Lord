
# Warrior
• Attributes: Increase two scores by 1. 
• Defense: +1; Health: +10
*Rage:* As long as you have taken damage or made an attack the previous round or sooner, you have Rage. If you spend one entire round without taking damage or making an attack, you lose rage.

### Level 1
Combat Training: You roll to attack with 1 boon. When you get a critical success on such a roll, the attack deals an extra 1d6 damage.

Taunt (Spell): If you have Rage, target one creature you can see within your reach. It makes a will roll, and on a failure it must use its next action to make a melee attack against you.

### Level 2
Combat Prowess: Your attacks with weapons deal 1d6 extra damage.

Heroic Strike (Spell): If you have Rage, make a melee attack with 1 boon. If you hit, all creatures within 5 yards gain 1 bane on any attack they make against a target other than you. (Luck Ends)

# Mage
* Attributes: Increase two scores by 1. 
* Mana: 4; Health: +4
*Mana:* As a Free Action, you may spend 1 point of Mana to restore all castings to one of your spells.

### Level 1
Fireball (Spell): A ball of fire rushes from your hands, dealing 3d6 damage to everything in a 2-yard sphere within medium range. Each creature in the area takes half the damage with a success on an Agility roll.

Frost Armor (Spell): A shield of ice surrounds you and moves with you. The field has Health 10. For the duration, when you would take damage, the shield takes the damage instead. If the shield’s damage total equals its Health, the effect ends immediately. This effect lasts 1 minute.

## Level 2
Arcane Missiles (Spell): Seven magical darts fly from your fingertip, divided as you choose among up to three creatures or objects within long range. Each dart automatically hits, provided there is an unobstructed path between you and the target. A target takes 1 damage for each dart that hits it.

Frost Bolt (Spell): Target creature within medium range takes 1d6 damage and must make an Agility roll. On a failure it is held and it takes an additional 1d6 damage.
